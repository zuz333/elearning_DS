Module 1

What is DILAPAN-S?

DILAPAN-S is a hygroscopic cervical dilator used for cervical ripening and dilation. It is designed for use by healthcare professionals trained in obstetrics and gynaecology.

  • Mechanical, non-pharmacological cervical ripening
  • Hydrogel-based dilating part
  • Used according to the local IFU and hospital protocol

Module 2

How DILAPAN-S works

The dilating part absorbs moisture from the cervical canal and gradually increases in diameter. This mechanical action supports cervical softening and dilation over time.
